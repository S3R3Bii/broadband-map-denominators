## Exact diagnostic and sensitivity

For a fixed state/speed and adjacent source dates, let N0,N1 be published total units and p0,p1 the published fraction served. Define I0=N0*p0 and I1=N1*p1 as fraction-implied served-unit quantities, not exact observed integer served counts. Preserve this distinction in every output and paper. Calculate the reported share change in percentage points as 100*(p1-p0), the unit change N1-N0 and its relative value, and the symmetric arithmetic decomposition:

S=50*(I1-I0)*(1/N0+1/N1)
D=50*(I0+I1)*(1/N1-1/N0)
S+D=100*(p1-p0).

S is the fraction-implied numerator component and D the denominator component under this fixed symmetric convention. This exact identity is mathematical, not a newly discovered causal law. The symmetric convention averages the two orders of replacing numerator and denominator; it is chosen before values, not fitted for a favorable outcome. Neither component identifies constructed infrastructure, corrected provider submissions, changed unit classifications, map errors or new customer connections. Publish the signed components and complete native facts, not a causal split or realized ROI.

Also calculate an explicitly authored constant-baseline-implied-count counterfactual p_cf=I0/N1. Report p_cf-p0 in percentage points only if I0<=N1; otherwise report infeasible, without clipping or silently changing the count. This holds the baseline fraction-implied quantity fixed while changing the denominator; it is not an observed no-construction world. Preserve infeasibility separately from zero effect.

Retain a fixed numerical sensitivity of plus/minus 0.0001 fraction units (0.01 percentage points), clipped to [0,1], around each published source share. This is an authored one-unit-of-the-specification's-last-decimal-place perturbation, not a publisher-certified rounding bound, sampling confidence interval or model of source error. Retain actual native precision even if it exceeds the example's four decimals; do not round the original to force the assumption. Enumerate all four endpoint combinations for each pair, using exact rational arithmetic. Recompute share change and both symmetric components; report exact minima/maxima and whether sign is stable under this declared perturbation. Shared inputs are perturbed consistently. Do not assign scenario probabilities, imply independent observations or expand the sensitivity after seeing results.

No ML, fitting, seed, imputation, smoothing, operating threshold, p-value or cost assumption is needed for this arithmetic/identification question. Report every state/speed/transition, all source key exclusions, version/revision metadata, component distributions, counterfactual feasibility and sensitivity sign stability. Do not infer a denominator share of gain when the observed change is zero or opposite-signed; preserve exact signed components without ratios that hide cancellation.
