# Public-summary boundary

Source: FCC Broadband Data Collection, Fixed Broadband Summary by Geography Type. The public map's About / License and Attribution section says BDC availability and Census data presented there are free and not subject to copyright restriction, and suggests source attribution: https://broadbandmap.fcc.gov/about?version=jun2026 . This study publishes only a minimal attributed aggregate projection and clearly labeled new calculations after source checks; no FCC endorsement is implied.

The same notice explicitly reserves CostQuest/third-party rights in location-level Fabric data, including each location's ID, coordinates, address, unit count and building code. Those records are excluded. No Fabric license, account or download is requested. Aggregate total_units in a public availability summary is retained as the publisher's aggregate denominator, not repackaged into individual Fabric records or represented as a household-adoption count. Full source ZIPs, publisher documents/pages, logos, contacts, private records and formal-publication drafts stay private. Any marked restriction discovered in source archives is a gate, not overridden by the availability-data notice. The authored-code license is not blanket relicensing of third-party material.
